# Code of Conduct

AedwenOS is a small project made by people who give their time to it. We want
taking part to be a good experience for everyone, whatever their background or
experience level.

This code of conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
which is licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps the community:

- Being kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully. Review the work, not
  the person.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Putting the project and its users first, not personal preference.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks.
- Harassment, in public or private.
- Publishing other people's private information, such as an email or physical
  address, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: the issue tracker, pull requests,
discussions, and any other place where someone represents the project in
public.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards, and will take fair and appropriate action in response to behaviour
they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. That can mean
removing, editing or rejecting comments, commits, issues and other
contributions, and temporarily or permanently banning a person from the
project.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, [@SECT19N](https://github.com/SECT19N),
privately through the contact options on their GitHub profile, or use GitHub's
"Report content" option on the comment or issue. Please do not report conduct
problems in a public issue. Reports are handled in confidence, and the people
involved are treated with respect and fairness.

Enforcement follows the
[Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
a private correction, a warning, a temporary ban, then a permanent ban,
depending on the severity and pattern of the behaviour.
